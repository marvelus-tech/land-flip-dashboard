# Vacant Lot Seller Sourcing — Wiener Bros / Joe McCall Masterclass

Source: https://youtu.be/cWcOwKCG-Ic (Video 4 — Finding Sellers)

## Core Philosophy
- Land flipping is a **marketing business**, not a real estate business.
- Four things: marketing → talk to sellers → make offers → follow up.
- Pipeline must stay on; never turn off acquisitions.
- Money is in the follow-up: 80% of deals came from follow-up in one 58-deal study.

## Propwire Filter Settings (Primary List Source)

### Example County
- Lee County, Florida (used because high buyer concentration from prior buyer research)

### Property Type
- Land / Vacant Lots
- 139,000 vacant land properties in Lee County at time of video

### Size Filter
- Minimum: **0.2 acres**
- Maximum: **1 acre**
- Rationale: quarter-acre lots are actively bought/sold; avoid tiny lots

### Owner Filters
- **Out-of-state owners** (out-of-county also possible in some tools)
- **Owned > 10 years**
- Rationale: emotionally removed, more open to discount, not a recent purchase

### Financial Filters
- **Tax delinquent year 2020** (or last 3 years)
- Rationale: inability to pay small tax bill signals distress / motivation

### Resulting Lead Counts
- Land only + 0.2–1 acre + out-of-state + >10 years: ~25,000
- Adding tax delinquent 2020: ~9,600

### Geographic Filtering
- Filter by **top zip codes** from buyer research (e.g., top 3 zip codes)
- Reduces list to hot areas instead of random sampling

## On-Market / MLS Lead Method

### Propwire MLS Filters
- Active on MLS
- Days on market **> 180 days** (6 months)
- Combine with: out-of-state, >10 years, vacant land
- Result: ~489 listings (example)

### Redfin / Public Portals
- Search vacant lots with keywords: **"motivated"**
- Filter by >30 days, >60 days, price reduced
- Look for listing descriptions with seller story/motivation
- Find listing agent email/phone and contact them directly

## Outreach & Marketing Methods

### Direct Mail
- **500 postcards per week** (example cadence)
- Mail pieces use a **reference number** instead of APN/GPS coordinates
- Include a **24-hour recorded voicemail** number
- Seller calls voicemail, leaves name/number/reference ID
- Response rate: ~1%
- 1 out of 25–30 offers accepted on average

### Postcard / Letter Content
- Reference number at top
- "If you're interested in selling, I might make an offer. Text or call 24-hour recorded voicemail."
- Voicemail: ask for name, number, reference ID; promise offer in a couple days

### Cold Calling
- Use **triple dialers** or one-by-one dialers
- VAs handle calling
- 4–5 hours of cold calling per VA → 6–7 leads/day for land (vs 1–2 for houses)
- Numbers are currently very good because fewer people call landowners

### Texting
- Mentioned as part of the marketing mix
- Used alongside direct mail and cold calling

### Email to Listing Agents (On-Market)
- Use Propwire to get realtor email/phone from MLS listing
- Email: "I see you've had this listed for 6 months. I'm an unrepresented investor. How negotiable is the seller? I can pay more with seller financing."
- Position as: agent can represent you and get both sides of commission
- Ask the million-dollar question: **"Do you have any other properties?"**

### Skip Tracing
- Download list from Propwire
- Skip trace to get owner phone numbers
- Also reveals entities with many properties (e.g., LLC with 407 lots) who can become buyers, lenders, or sellers

## Key Metrics & Workflow

### Daily/Weekly Targets
- **5 new offers per day** (25/week, 100/month)
- 100 offers/month → you will do deals
- Break down monetary goal into daily controllable activities

### Lead-to-Deal Math
- ~25–30 offers to do one deal
- ~30 leads to make 25 offers
- Direct mail: ~1% response rate
- 500 postcards/week example
- Direct mail leads are higher quality than cold-call leads (seller inbound)

### Follow-Up Discipline
- Average deal requires **6–7 touches** over **2–3 months**
- 80% of sales happen between **5th and 12th follow-up**
- Most people stop before the 5th follow-up

### Outsourcing
- Hire VAs for $5/hour activities (cold calling, email to agents, list building)
- Use mail company for direct mail
- Focus on higher-value activities: talking to sellers, making offers, structuring deals

## Offer Positioning

### Cash Offer + Owner Financing Offer
- Lead with low cash offer (where you can flip it)
- If rejected, offer more if seller will carry financing
- Typical terms: 10% down, 4–5% interest, 5 years
- Payment may be ~$100/month for small lots

### Agent Relationship
- Tell agent they can represent you and get full commission
- For low offers, frame as: "This will help the seller get a price reduction and help you sell it"
- Always reassure agent how they get paid
