🚨 Tax Crisis Deep Dive — Nevada Property Tax & Nye County

Nevada has no state income tax, but property taxes are not zero. Nye County assesses property at 35% of taxable value, with a tax rate around $2.50–$3.00 per $100 assessed. For a $50K parcel, that means $400–$600/year. For retirees on fixed income or heirs who inherited, even "low" taxes become a burden when the land produces zero income and has no development plan.
